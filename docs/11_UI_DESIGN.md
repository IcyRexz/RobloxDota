# UI design system

Owner decision 2026-10-21: **this is the one design standard for every piece of UI in the game.** New UI follows it; existing UI was restyled to it. The values live in code in `src/shared/UiTheme.luau`. Scripts read them from there and never hard-code their own fonts or colours. Change the theme file and everything follows.

Goal: minimal, clean and readable mid-fight. Few shapes, no outlines, and one accent, so the battlefield stays the focus.

## The six rules
1. **One font: Builder Sans** (Roblox's own UI font, built in). Weight and size carry the hierarchy, never a second font.
2. **One surface:** a see-through dark panel (`Surface`, 62% opaque on the HUD, 82% for full menus). No thick borders, no outlines, small consistent corner rounding (8 px; 12 px for whole menus).
3. **One accent: gold** (plus orange, `UiTheme.Improves`, used only for the "Improves: ..." line on mastery and upgrade tiles; owner 2026-10-21), gold only for progress and rewards: level, XP, Gold, masteries taken, the hovered mastery tile, and the pending-pick hint. Everything else is **white at three strengths**: full for values, about 65% for labels, about 40% for captions, locked items and hints.
4. **Colours with a fixed meaning only:** green = friendly health, red = enemy health (and damage you take). No decorative colours: no purple badges, no blue outlines, no team-coloured banners.
5. **Thin bars, label above:** health 8 px, XP 3 px, unit 4 px. Names and numbers sit above or beside the bar, never inside it. No segment ticks. **Exception, structure bars (owner 2026-10-21):** towers, barracks and the Base Core get big bars like the MOBA-era ones, a bit larger: a 200 px wide, 18 px bar with "current / max" centred inside it (Body Bold, floating shadow) and the name above in Title Bold (`StructureBars.client.luau`).
6. **Spacing in steps of 4 px** (`UiTheme.space(n)`).

## Tokens (`src/shared/UiTheme.luau`)
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `Font.Regular / Medium / Bold / Heavy` | Builder Sans 400 / 500 / 700 / 800 | Body / labels / values / headings and names |
| `TextSize` | Caption 11, Small 12, Body 14, Value 16, Title 19, Heading 24 | |
| `Surface` | RGB 12, 14, 18 | Every panel |
| `SurfaceTransparency` / `MenuTransparency` | 0.38 / 0.18 | HUD pieces / full menus |
| `TrackTransparency` | 0.45 | Empty part of a bar |
| `Raised` at `RaisedTransparency` 0.94 (hover 0.90, disabled 0.96) | white 6% (10%, 4%) | Cards and slots inside a menu |
| `Radius` / `RadiusLarge` | 8 / 12 px | |
| `Scrim` at 0.5 | black 50% | Dims the game behind a menu |
| `Text` with `Strength.Value / Label / Faint` | white at TextTransparency 0 / 0.35 / 0.6 | |
| `Gold` | RGB 245, 196, 81 | The accent |
| `OnGold` | RGB 26, 20, 8 | Text on a gold fill |
| `Ally` / `Enemy` | RGB 94, 211, 126 / 229, 83, 75 | Health meaning only |
| `Bar.Health / Xp / Structure / Unit` | 8 / 3 / 18 / 4 px | Structure was 5 until 2026-10-21 |
| `Scale` / `MenuScale` | 1.4 / 1.5 | Every screen element is drawn this much larger than its layout numbers (`UiTheme.applyScale`), growing around its anchor; menus still shrink to fit small screens. Layout numbers in this doc are at 1x. |

Helpers: `UiTheme.text`, `UiTheme.panel`, `UiTheme.bar`, `UiTheme.corner`, `UiTheme.space`, `UiTheme.floatingText` (soft dark shadow for text that floats over the world).

## Components
- **Bottom HUD** (`HeroHud.client.luau`): a level ring (52 px circle, surface fill, 2 px gold ring, "LV" caption above the number). The ring is a quiet white line normally and turns gold with a soft pulsing glow only while a mastery pick is waiting. To its right, a block 300 px wide: hero name (label strength) and "600 / 600" (value) above an 8 px health bar, then a 3 px gold XP bar with "xp / next XP" (caption, label strength) to its right. Under that, ability slots (40 px, surface, rounded; key hint top-left as a caption; names shrink to fit). **Cooldown: a circular sweep** (`UiTheme.radialCover`): a dark wedge that disappears like a clock hand, with the seconds in the centre. A pending mastery pick shows a gold **"MASTERY" pill** above the ring (a dark round key chip with a gold "U", then "MASTERY" or "MASTERY x2"), bobbing gently. Slots: BOLT (LMB), STRIKE (Q), TELEPORT (E). Under BOLT, small pips count towards the passive (white, all gold when the next hit is charged); BOLT gets a 2 px gold rim while a Charged Blink bolt is ready.
- **Text that can wrap never shrinks or overlaps** (owner 2026-10-22): any label that may run to two lines (names, "Improves" lines, descriptions) wraps and grows to fit its lines inside a vertical list (MenuKit `flow`), so a second line moves what follows down. If the container can run out of room it scrolls (info area) or clips at its own edge (tiles, above the corner line). Do not use TextScaled to make long text fit; one-line labels (stat rows, slot tags) keep their fixed heights.
- **Full menus share one layout: `src/shared/MenuKit.luau`** (owner 2026-10-21: the Base Upgrades page must be exactly like the Character Menu). Scrim, one 900 x 560 menu surface (radius 12) at MenuScale, two columns. **Left (~34%)** has one of two tops. **Profile top** (Character Menu): a 48 px gold ring (small caption over a value), Title, a label-strength subtitle, a 3 px gold bar and a faint label under it, then the stat rows, then a caption and a row of 40 px slots (selected = 1 px gold line). **Picker top** (Base Upgrades; owner 2026-10-21: bigger building icons, no repeated names): a caption and full-width 52 px cards (raised white 6%, radius 10; 26 px icon, name in Value Heavy, a short detail on the right; selected = solid 1 px gold line and gold detail, hover = faint line), then the stat rows; no slot row. Both: a caption and five label-left / value-right rows (improved values in gold), and the **info area** (raised white 6%, radius 10, clipped) filling the rest: title (Title Heavy), an orange "Improves: ..." line when relevant (Body), and body text at label strength (Body, fixed), stacked; when they are taller than the area it scrolls (3 px faint bar, `UiTheme.ScrollBar`) and jumps back to the top when something new is shown (owner 2026-10-21: it used to shrink to fit and became unreadable; info text is never smaller than the tile text). Hovering a slot or tile fills it and it keeps the last thing shown; **no floating tooltips**. **Right (~66%):** Heading, a line under it (gold when something can be done), pips top right (gold = done, half gold = waiting), and a 3-across grid of tiles (raised white 6%, radius 10, 116 px tall): name (Body Heavy), **"Improves: ..." in orange** (`UiTheme.Improves`), short text, and a bottom line (TAKEN / OWNED in gold, or a price in gold, enemy red when you cannot afford it). Tile looks: **taken** = lit, solid gold line, gold name; **open** = normal, hover gold line and gold name; **locked** = disabled transparency, faint. A faint close hint bottom right.
- **Base Upgrades menu** (`BaseUpgradeMenu.client.luau`, MenuKit picker top, opened only from the Upgrade Workbench prompt): UPGRADES cards (owner 2026-10-21: 🏰 Buildings, ⚔️ Melee Units, 🏹 Archers, each "N/6"); then what the selected category improves as stat rows: BUILDINGS (Tower Health, Tower Damage, Tower Attack Speed, Tower Range, Barracks Health, Core Health) or SWORDSMAN / ARCHER (health, damage, attack speed as "1/s", range, and "Per barracks: N every 15s", Reinforcements included), all with upgrades and army buffs. Right: the category name as the heading, the line under it shows your Gold in gold (or the server reply for 2.5 s: Not Enough Gold in enemy red), pips = upgrades owned, tiles = the category's six upgrades, and the **info area under the tiles** (owner 2026-10-21; MenuKit `infoUnderGrid`). **Large text** (owner 2026-10-21, MenuKit `largeText`): 60 px cards with 30 px icons, names in Title Heavy (shrinking to Body if too long) and details in Value; 128 px tiles with name in Value Heavy, "Improves" in Small, text in Body - 1, price in Body; stat rows 36 px in Value; info title in Title, "Improves" line and body in Value (owner 2026-10-21: body was too small). "F to close". Structure name labels show the building icon and its name (both barracks read "Barracks"; no upgrade count since upgrades are no longer per building); the workbench has a floating "Upgrade Workbench" label (18 px Bold, 1.5x the Small size; owner 2026-10-21).
- **Party list and Tab scoreboard** (`Scoreboard.client.luau`, top left, owner 2026-10-21, Dota-like): always on, every player stacked vertically (you first): a 44 px round portrait (headshot, or the initial until it loads), the level in an 18 px surface circle with a gold ring on the portrait's lower edge, and a 4 px green health bar under it; the portrait greys out while dead. Holding **Tab** swaps it for the scoreboard: a menu surface (radius 12) with faint captions PLAYER / LV / KILLS / MASTERIES / GOLD and one 40 px row per player (30 px portrait, name in Body Heavy over the class at label strength, level in gold, kills, one 22 px chip per mastery level, 6 px apart, with the picked mastery's initials in gold and a gold line, an empty slot with a faint white outline while still open, Gold in gold); your row is lit (raised white 10%). Spacing (owner 2026-10-21): 16 px panel padding, 12 px row inset on both ends, an even 24 px gap between columns; the board is drawn at the HUD scale and shrinks to stay 12 px inside narrow or short screens; long names are cut with "…". Roblox's own player list is turned off.
- **Target panel** (`TargetInfo.client.luau`, top centre, flush near the top): what you are aiming at (the enemy under the red marker). A surface panel with a round 44 px portrait (a 3D view of the unit's head; buildings show a short tag), the name and "current / max" above an 8 px health bar (enemy red, ally green). Lingers 0.6 s after you look away, then fades.
- **Overhead bars** (other players, structures, minions): name in floating white text above a thin bar (5 px structures, 4 px units/heroes), ally green or enemy red. No numbers inside, no ticks.
- **Gold counter** (flush in the window's top-right corner, 12 px in, over the top bar area): a surface pill with a 12 px gold dot and the amount in Heavy 16. Earned gold pops a floating gold "+N" from the kill.
- **Developer panel** (owner 2026-10-21; Studio only, `DevPanel.client.luau`): a surface panel 8 px under the Gold counter, right-aligned with it, rows with a faint caption and raised Body Bold buttons: DEV "+30 XP" (white) "+200 Gold" (gold); SPAWN Zombie / Brute / Spitter (enemy red) Swordsman / Archer (ally green); EVENT "Next event"; a faint hint line. ` (backquote) frees the first-person mouse to click it (a Modal button) and locks it again. Amounts and kinds in `Config.Dev`; the server (`DevTools.server.luau`) ignores the commands outside Studio.
- **Event banners** use the announcement banner: headline = the event name in capitals (e.g. "ZOMBIE SURGE"), subline = its text from `Config.Events`; end: "Zombie Surge is over" / "Zombies are back to normal".
- **Character Menu** (`CharacterMenu.client.luau`, MenuKit, U): ring = level ("LV"), title = player name, subtitle = class, bar = XP; ATTRIBUTES (Health, Health Regen, Damage, Move Speed, Attack Speed, Range); ABILITIES (BOLT, PASSIVE, STRIKE, TELEPORT, BANNER); info area. Right: "Masteries", the line under it (gold "1 available · pick one", else "Next at level N"), five pips, the 3x3 mastery grid (no row categories; each tile's orange line says what it improves). "U to close".
- **Banners** (`Announcements.client.luau`): a surface pill at the upper middle, headline Heavy 22 in white, subline at label strength. No team colours.
- **Crosshair**: white, turning enemy red exactly while the red target marker is on (a click would hit).
- **Minimap**: the map inside a surface panel (radius 8); dots use ally green and enemy red; you are a white dot with a facing tick.
- **Floating text** (`src/shared/FloatingText.luau`, damage numbers and "+1" gold): Builder Sans Heavy with a soft shadow; pops in slightly small, springs to 125% and settles (0.3 s), holds 0.45 s, then drifts up and fades (0.9 s). White for damage you deal, enemy red for damage you take, gold for gold; bigger hits get bigger numbers.

## Not covered: things in the game world
Effects that are part of the world rather than the interface keep their own colours: the lightning bolt, arrows, tower shots, the red target ring on the ground, ability area markers, and the minimap's muted picture of the ground, fences, trees and rocks. Area markers on the ground use `src/shared/GroundRing.luau` (a dashed rim and faint fill whose pieces follow the ground's shape). Anything drawn as interface on top of the game (text, bars, panels, buttons, markers on the minimap) follows this system.

## Writing
Plain words, sentence case for sentences, short labels. Stats are named plainly: Health, Health Regen, Damage, Move Speed, Attack Speed, Range. Upgrades are **masteries**. Captions are short and may be uppercase (ATTRIBUTES).

## Process
- New or changed UI must use `UiTheme`: no raw fonts, colours or radii in UI scripts.
- If something truly needs a new token, add it to `UiTheme.luau` and to this doc in the same change, and record why in `TASK_STATUS.md`.
