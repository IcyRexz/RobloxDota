# Scoped game design document

All numerical values below are starting hypotheses, not validated balance. Keep them in `src/shared/Config.lua` as systems are implemented.

## Match and map
Two player slots: Blue and Red (fixed world colors; owner decision 2026-10-12), also identified by labels/icons. Both use Zeus (was Spark). One straight lane about 160 studs long and 36 studs wide (widened from 28 on 2026-10-13), symmetrical cover-free combat space, fenced edges, no vertical routes. Cores at lane ends, each tower about 32 studs in front of its core, with a barracks between tower and core; protected spawn pads behind cores. Current graybox layout (lane along X, Blue at negative X, mirrored for Red): walled arena x -100..100, z -18..18; spawn pad x ±92 (16×16); core x ±72 (10×10, 12 tall); barracks x ±56 (8×8, 8 tall); tower x ±40 (8×8, 16 tall). Source: `src/map/`. Trees outside the walls are decoration only.

Use a fixed elevated follow camera (Mobile Legends style, owner decision 2026-10-15: 55 degrees down, 70 studs back, FOV 40, smooth follow, never rotates; it looks down the lane toward the enemy, so each side sees its own base at the bottom of the screen, a deviation from the earlier 'same world orientation for both sides'; same camera on desktop and phones, Roblox shift lock disabled; `src/client/HeroCamera.client.luau`), and conspicuous friendly/enemy objective labels. Native thumbstick motion follows that camera. Disable jump (character jump power 0 in the project file), climbing and swimming; constrain the arena so these states cannot be entered. Keep characters visually consistent through an R15 template or controlled appearance. Do not let arbitrary avatar proportions alter hitboxes.

State flow: Waiting -> Countdown -> Playing -> Results -> Resetting -> Waiting.
Both players tap Ready. Five-second countdown; any departure cancels it. Start health/cooldowns at clean values; spawn first wave when Playing begins. Match clock runs only during Playing. At six minutes, higher remaining core health percentage wins; ties use tower health percentage, then barracks health percentage; exact ties are draws. Kills never break a tie. A destroyed core ends the match immediately. Evaluate simultaneous objective deaths within the same simulation step as a draw; commit one result only.

During Playing a departure ends the match as a forfeit win for the remaining participant. Before Playing it returns to Waiting. No reconnect/resume within a match. New arrivals during Results wait for the next match, never inherit the prior player's slot/state. Results displays outcome and reason. Each player can tap Rematch; when both accept, fully reset and start a new countdown. After 30 seconds without both accepting, reset to Waiting. A lone player sees 'Waiting for opponent'. A replacement player must Ready. Configure maximum server size to two; no cross-server matchmaker. Testers join a friend's server through Roblox's permitted join flow.

## Hero: Zeus
(Renamed from Spark, owner decision 2026-10-15; same kit for now.) Every player's character is the hero: the server marks it `Kind = Hero` with the player's team and owns its Health/MaxHealth attributes (Roblox's own health regeneration is removed). Minions and towers target it. Minions are scaled to 70% so heroes stand taller. HUD (owner decision 2026-10-15: centred): bottom centre, a level badge (placeholder "LV 1"; levels are KIV), a health bar with the hero name and "current / max", a thin xp bar (placeholder), and four skill slots (Attack live; Skill 1, Skill 2, Ult locked placeholders) with cooldown covers driven by server-time `<Action>ReadyAt` attributes; a name and segmented health bar over every hero's head (green ally, red enemy); Roblox's default health bar and name tags hidden. Desktop test control for Pulse: left mouse button (hold repeats) or click the Attack slot. Targeting (Mobile Legends style, owner decision 2026-10-15): the client marks the target it expects (spinning ring + outline; red when within range, orange when within the 40-stud acquire range) using the same priority, keeps it while valid, and on attack walks the hero into range (manual steering cancels), turns and plays the swing locally, then sends the marked target as a hint. The server uses the hint only if it passes every check, else picks its own. Feedback: lightning bolt from Zeus's hand, white flash on the target, damage numbers (white dealt by you, red taken). The server never moves or turns the player's character. Picking a target (owner request 2026-10-15): click or tap an enemy within the acquire range to lock onto it (still, solid ring), Tab cycles through enemies in range nearest first; the lock ends when the target dies or leaves the acquire range, or on a click on empty ground. Health bars of minions, heroes and structures show within 60 studs of the viewer's hero (`Config.Display.HealthBarRange`, measured from the hero, not the camera), always beyond the 40-stud acquire range, so anything targetable has a visible bar. Phone layout decided with touch controls.
Health 600; movement speed 16 studs/second; no mana, passive regeneration, critical hits, armor, random damage, stun or item modifiers. Restore health on respawn only. Death disables abilities and movement, plays death animation, then respawns after five seconds. Spawn protection lasts two seconds and ends immediately if the player makes an accepted attack/cast; protected heroes cannot deal damage without dropping protection. Dash is allowed and does not remove protection.

Three right-thumb actions:
- **Pulse (basic attack):** tap once to attack; hold repeats at most once per 0.75 seconds. Range 24 studs, 40 damage, 0.15-second windup. Server auto-selects nearest eligible enemy hero, else minion, else unlocked structure in range and line of sight. Ties use stable entity ID. No target means no cooldown. Recheck eligibility at impact; if target left range/line of sight, miss and retain spent cooldown. No homing physical projectile is required: use an authoritative hit and cosmetic tracer.
- **Arc Bolt:** 6-second cooldown, 100 damage, 40-stud maximum travel, 80 studs/second, 0.20-second windup, collision radius 2 studs. Press-drag-release to aim; tap uses nearest eligible enemy direction or current facing. Stop at the first enemy or solid obstruction; no piercing, ally damage or core damage while locked. Use server swept collision, not client touch reports. Valid release consumes cooldown even if it misses.
- **Slip (dash):** 8-second cooldown, up to 12 studs over 0.18 seconds, no damage/invulnerability. Tap uses current movement direction, else facing. Direction is planar. Server clamps distance, sweeps against walls, checks arena limits and issues a bounded movement authorization. No wall crossing, knockback, attack reset or target lock.

Starting an action sets its cooldown immediately on server acceptance. Pulse and Bolt windups cannot overlap; dash is rejected during a windup. Pulse/Bolt are rejected during dash. Death cancels pending impacts/projectiles from that hero, and rematch cancels every prior-match task. Holding Pulse must not queue attacks for later execution while another action is locked. Visual hit reactions never add gameplay stun.

## Lane systems
Each side spawns a wave of four melee minions (sword) and one archer (bow) every 25 seconds (owner decision 2026-10-13; was three identical minions every 15 seconds). Minions are the default Roblox R15 character (blocky default look, no player avatar), body-colored by team, and spawn at the `MinionSpawn` marker just in front of their own core (owner decision 2026-10-12). Their waypoints must route around their own barracks and tower, which sit on the lane centerline. Melee minion: health 120, damage 12, range 5 studs, aggro 8. Archer: health 80, damage 10, range 16 studs, aggro 16. Both: attack interval 1 second, movement speed 8, turn rate 270 degrees/second (they turn smoothly toward where they walk or the enemy they fight instead of snapping; owner request 2026-10-15). Max 30 living minions across both sides (raised from 24; verify on the baseline phone in T22). If a wave would exceed that cap, spawn only available slots, alternating side priority each wave; skip remaining units, never build a backlog. Despawn units after 180 seconds (raised from 60 on 2026-10-15 so a winning wave can push to the core) or at match end. Weapons are simple block placeholders for now. Archer shots: 0.3-second bow draw, then an arrow flies at 60 studs/second and the damage lands on arrival; an archer killed mid-draw never releases. Each minion shows a small nameless health bar (green ally / red enemy), drawn per player (`src/client/MinionVisuals.client.luau`), which also draws the bow pose and arrow (display only; the server schedules the hit).

The first wave spawns 5 seconds after match start so heroes can reach the lane, then every 25 seconds (owner decision 2026-10-13, after MLBB's 0:10 first wave).

Minions follow fixed lane waypoints. When choosing a new target they take, in order: the nearest enemy minion within their aggro range, otherwise the nearest unprotected enemy hero within aggro range, otherwise the nearest enemy structure within aggro range (measured to its side) that can currently take damage. Because they walk down the lane, they meet the tower, then the barracks, then the core (owner decision 2026-10-15: no separate ordering logic). Destroyed structures disappear (owner decision 2026-10-15) and the route continues through the enemy base to the core. Every player then sees a Mobile Legends-style banner centred near the top of the screen: a strip in the scoring team's color, bold white text "THE RED TEAM HAS LOST THEIR TOWER" (team name tinted; the barracks is announced as the inhibitor) and a smaller line: for an inhibitor "BLUE MINIONS ARE NOW STRONGER" (the scoring team), otherwise "ENEMY TOWER DESTROYED" / "YOUR TOWER HAS FALLEN" for the viewer; banners queue (`src/client/Announcements.client.luau`, from the server's `StructureDestroyed` event). Locked structures (barracks before its tower falls, core before its barracks falls) are walked past, not attacked. Ties use stable entity ID. No pathfinding service dependency on this flat lane. Minions and towers do not target protected heroes.

Target rules (owner decision 2026-10-13, adapted from observed MLBB behavior):
- **Sticky unit targets:** a minion keeps its minion/hero target until it dies, becomes protected or moves more than 2 studs beyond the minion's aggro range. Then it picks again.
- **Structure lock:** once a minion starts hitting a structure, it keeps hitting that structure until the structure falls or the minion dies. Enemy minions merely arriving do not pull it off, but an enemy minion that hits it does: the minion fights back against that attacker while it stays within 20 studs (`RetaliateRange`), then returns to its normal targets (owner decision 2026-10-15). This retaliation applies whatever the minion was doing.
- **Leash:** when a minion leaves its lane path to chase a unit, it records where it left (the leash anchor). If it gets more than 12 studs from the anchor, or chases for 3 seconds without being in attack range, it drops the target and walks back to the anchor, ignoring all targets until it is within 2 studs of it. Then it continues along its waypoints.
- After the enemy tower falls, the route continues past it to the enemy barracks and then the core.

Tower health 900, range 28 studs, 60 damage every 1 second. Prefer enemy minions, then enemy hero; retain an eligible target until it dies/leaves range/protection changes. Exception (owner plan 2026-10-14): if an enemy hero in range damages an allied hero, the tower switches to that enemy hero. Each shot is a glowing orb (team color) fired from the tip of the tower knight's sword at 80 studs/second; damage lands on arrival, so towers can take last hits. Only enemy towers show their attack range (a player's own tower can never hit them): a faint grey dotted circle on the floor, drawn per player, that appears when the viewer comes within 20 studs of its edge and turns solid red while that tower is aiming at the viewer's character (owner decision 2026-10-15, MOBA convention). (`src/server/Structures.server.luau`; drawn by `src/client/TowerEffects.client.luau`; sword-tip `Muzzle` attachment placed by `src/server/TowerVisuals.server.luau`.) Rules adapted from MLBB turrets (owner decision 2026-10-13):
- **Ramp on heroes:** each hit in a row on the same hero does more damage: 60, 90, 120, then 120 per hit after that. The ramp resets when the tower switches target or 2 seconds pass without it hitting that hero. Minions always take 60.
- **Backdoor protection:** tower, barracks and core take 50% less damage from heroes while no minion of the attacking team is within 28 studs of that structure. Minion damage is never reduced. A hero can still damage a structure alone, just slowly, while taking tower fire. Core health 1200; it does not attack. Objectives fall in a fixed order (owner decision 2026-10-15): tower, then barracks (the inhibitor), then core. Barracks (owner decision 2026-10-12): health 700; does not attack; cannot be damaged until its own team's tower is destroyed. The core cannot take any damage, including bolt or minion hits, until its own team's barracks is destroyed. When a team destroys the enemy barracks, that team's minions spawned afterwards get +25% health and damage for the rest of the match (was +50%; owner decision 2026-10-15). All structures have a MOBA-style overhead bar drawn per player: name, health bar with a divider every 100 HP and "current / max" text; green for the viewer's team, red for the enemy; hidden beyond 60 studs from the viewer's character (`src/client/StructureBars.client.luau`, reading the server-owned Health/MaxHealth attributes). No tower or barracks respawn mid-match.

## Presentation and feedback
One-line objective: 'Push with your minions. Break the tower, then the inhibitor, then the core.' Display both core bars, own HP, clock, three actions, cooldown overlays, death countdown, result and rematch. Show an outline/ring for Pulse's current predicted target and aim line for Bolt, but treat these as predictions. Telegraph a bolt before release, use distinct team shapes/colors, and cap particles. A missed spell and a server rejection have distinct feedback. Disable buttons and targeting in non-playing states.

## What to show in the final demo
A real mobile duel: move while aiming, dodge a bolt, push with minions, destroy tower/core, see result, rematch. Briefly show one animation close-up and a test log entry demonstrating server rejection of invalid requests. Do not build mock ranked menus, fake player numbers, hero cards for unimplemented characters or store screens. Reuse a restrained visual style with clearly labeled placeholder art if needed.
