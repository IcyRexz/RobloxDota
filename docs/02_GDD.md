# Game design document

Co-op base-defense shooter (owner decision 2026-10-20; brief in `docs/01_BRIEF.md`). Numbers marked *(carried)* come from the MOBA prototype code in `src/shared/Config.luau` and are starting points only. Anything marked **OPEN** waits for an owner decision; do not invent it. Keep this file and `Config.luau` consistent as systems land.

## Structure of a session
Hub -> mission (Briefing -> Build phase -> Wave -> Build phase -> … -> Final wave) -> Results -> back to Hub.

- **Hub:** shared area in the same place as the mission arena (owner decision 2026-10-20). Players pick a hero, equip unlocked gear and stand on a queue pad (or similar) to start a mission. Map choice limited by class level.
- **Party size:** 1–5 players per mission. Server capacity 5 (to confirm when the hub is built; with one shared arena only one mission runs per server).
- **Mission state flow:** Hub -> Briefing (short countdown; leaving cancels) -> BuildPhase -> Wave -> BuildPhase -> … -> Results -> Resetting -> Hub. Every match has an epoch; delayed callbacks from an older epoch never act.
- **Win:** the final wave (full version: a boss) is cleared with the Base Core still standing.
- **Lose:** the Base Core is destroyed. Full party wipe: **OPEN**.
- **Leaving mid-mission:** the mission continues for the rest of the party; if the last player leaves it resets. Rewards for leavers: **OPEN**.

## Map layout (mission)
Open battlefield for swarm defence (owner redesign 2026-10-20, replacing the same-day two-lane map; `src/map/`). The map runs along X: our base at negative X is the bottom of the screen, zombies come from positive X at the top. Screen-left is -Z, screen-right is +Z. Simple low-poly graybox style.
- **Battlefield** (`Battlefield.model.json`): 423×420 studs (x -153..270, z -210..210); the base sits flush against its bottom edge (owner 2026-10-20). No lanes, no road strips, no arena walls. The fighting happens roughly between x -150 and 160, z ±130, so the edges are far from it.
- **Landscape** (owner 2026-10-20): rolling grass Terrain from `src/server/Landscape.luau`, at most 6 studs up and 4 down (current map -3.1 to +4.7), flat in and around the base (8-stud margin) and around the zombie cores and spawns (14 studs), easing into hills over 25 studs; our own value noise (fixed seed) instead of `math.noise`, so it can be reproduced outside Roblox. Rojo cannot sync terrain: baking stamps the terrain with a `LandscapeKey` attribute (saved with the place) and the server skips rebuilding when the key matches (rebuilding at every start made clients re-download the ground and stutter, owner report 2026-10-21); otherwise the server builds it at start. The owner runs `require(game.ServerScriptService.Server.Landscape).buildTerrain()` once in the Studio Command Bar (edit mode) so it shows and saves in the place. The `Ground` part is now an invisible, non-colliding bounds marker (minimap, terrain extent). Hills never block attacks (owner). **Trees and rocks are map parts** in `src/map/Nature.model.json` (visible in edit view like the towers), written by `tools/gen_nature.py`, which copies the height formula exactly and stores sample heights that the server checks at start (Output warning if they drift): 251 green placeholder trees (trunk + ball; a one-row forest belt on the north, south and east edges without shadows, plus 14 scattered) and 16 grey placeholder rocks, scattered ones at least 24 studs apart (never corridors). Units walk around trunks and rocks.
- **Boundary:** the base's back fence is the bottom edge; TEMPORARY invisible walls on the other edges and just behind the back wall (`Kind = Boundary`) until natural edges are built (T03a).
- **Base** (`PlayerBase.model.json`, bottom half): stone floor x -150..-70, z -66..66, enclosed by **fences** 9 tall, taller than a character and above jump height (owner 2026-10-20, replacing 8-tall walls; cannot be broken; attacks pass through them). Each fence segment is a collision part (a see-through dark slab in Studio's edit view as a placeholder, hidden in play) (`Kind = Fence`; line of sight and the crosshair skip it via `Targeting.seeThrough`, since Roblox raycasts always hit colliding parts) dressed by `src/server/FenceVisuals.server.luau` with the owner's `assets/FenceModel.rbxmx` repeated along it, or wooden placeholder posts and rails until that file exists. A front fence facing the enemy covers the middle (z -34..34); back and side fences close the rest, leaving two **broad openings** (32 studs each) at the front-left and front-right corners (z ±34..66). A tower guards each opening (`LeftTower` / `RightTower`, x -84, z ±50; 900 HP; knight art). Behind each tower an inhibitor (`LeftInhibitor` / `RightInhibitor`, Kind `Barracks`; x -104, z ±30; 700 HP). `BaseCore` in the centre (x -118, z 0; 1200 HP). Player spawn pad behind the core (x -138). Invisible `Opening` markers mark each gap for the swarm code.
- **Zombie side** (`ZombieBase.model.json`): two zombie spawning cores, `LeftZombieCore` and `RightZombieCore` (x 150, z ±70; 12×14×12; 1200 HP; dark green), spaced apart across the top. They do not attack.
- **Objective order** (each structure's `GuardedBy` attribute; rule in `Targeting.isLocked`): an inhibitor cannot be damaged while its tower stands; the Base Core cannot be damaged while **both** inhibitors stand. Zombie cores have no guards.
- **Unit spawns:** our minions spawn just inside the base in front of each inhibitor; zombies spawn in front of each zombie core, scattered within 7 studs. A spawn point stops once its source structure falls.
- **Camera:** first person, Roblox's built-in `LockFirstPerson` camera mode (set in `default.project.json`); mouse turns the view, cursor locked to the centre; crosshair in the centre, red over an attackable enemy (`src/client/Crosshair.client.luau`). Owner decisions 2026-10-20: fixed top-down camera removed, then default third person, then first person. No overhead bar on your own character. Shift lock disabled; jump allowed again (owner 2026-10-20; Roblox default jump power 50, about 7 studs high, below the 9-stud fences).
- **Minimap:** bottom-right, fixed orientation (base at the bottom, zombies from the top), shows fences, trees, rocks, structures, units and players (`src/client/Minimap.client.luau`).
- **Placeholders now in (2026-10-20):** forest belt on the edges (first pass of T03a; the invisible boundary stays behind it) and scattered trees/rocks plus hills (first pass of T03b). Final art, cliffs or mountains still to come. Compound build nodes are still planned (placement **OPEN**).

## Players and heroes
- Each player's Roblox avatar is their character (as in the prototype); the server marks it, owns Health/MaxHealth attributes and removes default regen *(carried: 600 HP, WalkSpeed 16, respawn 5 s)*.
- **Heroes (owner decision 2026-10-21, replacing classes):** a Dota-style roster; each hero has its own attack, abilities, talent pool and level. **Zeus** (lightning bolt) is the first and only hero so far. Roster size, roles (e.g. builder, healer, tank) and abilities **OPEN**.
- **Attack:** each hero has its own basic attack. PC: **aimed at the crosshair (owner 2026-10-20)**. Current Pulse attack: left click fires along the camera's look direction; the server casts the shot itself from the hero's head (sphere radius 1, 24 studs), hits the first enemy on that line, passes through allies, hills and fences, is stopped by trees, rocks and structures, and simply misses if nothing is in range. 40 damage (x Attack picks), 0.75 s cooldown spent on every shot, 0.15 s windup. No auto-walk. **Red marker = a click hits this (owner 2026-10-21):** the client runs the server's own shot rule (`Targeting.castShot`) along the crosshair every frame; if it would hit an enemy, that enemy gets the red ring + outline and the crosshair turns red, and the click fires straight. Only when the straight shot would miss does aim assist apply. **Aim assist (owner 2026-10-20):** an enemy within 24 studs, in line of sight and within 3° of the crosshair (measured to its body edge) gets the red ring + outline and shots aim straight at it; it stays until the crosshair is 5° off; move away and the outline and assist turn off. Click/Tab lock removed. The client only bends the direction it sends; the server still casts the shot. Ammo/mana/reload **OPEN**.
- **Death/respawn:** **OPEN** (respawn timer, revive, penalties).

## Enemies and waves
- **Current state (owner redesign 2026-10-20):** every 25 s (first at 5 s) each standing zombie core sends 12 **zombies** (8, then doubled to 16, then -25% to 12 by the owner on 2026-10-20) and each standing inhibitor sends our wave (4 melee + 1 archer, khaki). Zombies are all melee on the standard Roblox R15 body at full player size (was 70%, owner 2026-10-20): dark green skin, blood-red torso, muddy green legs, no face, glowing red eyes, both arms stretched forward. Attack: a **bite** (owner approved 2026-10-20, keep): the body leans back, then the upper body and head lunge forward; the shoulder also moves (arms alternate), played on each client from the server's `MinionSwing` event like the archer draw; the damage lands when the claw comes down (`ZombieSwipeHitDelay` 0.3 s) and a zombie killed mid-swing never lands it. Unit cap 100 (check frame rate). Inhibitor bonus (+25% on later spawns for the side that destroys one) still applies. **Sides (owner 2026-10-20):** just us and the zombies, no colour teams. Team attribute values come from `Config.Teams`: `Defenders` (players, our minions and structures; khaki) and `Zombies` (zombies and zombie cores; dark green). Banners say "WE LOST A …" / "ZOMBIES LOST A …". Health bars stay green for us and red for zombies (ally/enemy, not team colours).
- **Unit stats** (`Config.Minion`; order: health, attack, range, attack speed, movement speed, aggro):

  | Unit | Health | Attack | Range | Attack speed | Move speed | Aggro |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Zombie | 120 | 12 | 5 (melee) | 1 hit / 1 s | 8 | 14 |
  | Our melee minion | 173 | 17 | 5 (melee) | 1 hit / 1 s | 8 | 16 |
  | Our archer minion | 115 | 14 | 16 (arrow 60 studs/s) | 1 shot / 1 s (0.3 s draw) | 8 | 22 |

  Our minions were buffed +20% health and attack and given wider aggro on 2026-10-20 (owner: they were walking past zombies). Was melee 120 / 12 / aggro 8, archer 80 / 10 / aggro 16. Another +20% health and attack the same day (owner, after units went full size): melee 144/14 -> 173/17, archer 96/12 -> 115/14.

- **Zombie aggro (owner 2026-10-20):** a zombie goes for whatever enemy is **nearest** within its aggro range (14): our minion, a player or an attackable structure (measured to its side), re-checked every 0.2 s and kept unless something else is 2+ studs nearer. Zombies do **not** chase whoever hit them, call for help or lock onto a structure, so attacking a zombie does not make it follow you.
- **Our minions' aggro (standard rules, owner 2026-10-20):** a unit attacks, in order: whoever damaged it (enemy unit **or tower**), chasing while that attacker is within 32 studs (`RetaliateRange`, beyond tower range 28); the structure it is already hitting; the nearest enemy unit in aggro range; the nearest enemy hero in aggro range; the nearest unlocked enemy structure in aggro range (measured to its side). **Call for help:** when a unit is hit, same-team units within 10 studs (`HelpRange`) that are not already fighting back join in on the attacker.
- **Swarm movement** (`src/server/SwarmField.luau` + `Minions.server.luau`): no lanes or routes. The server keeps flow fields on a 4-stud grid (shortest walking distance around walls and structures, rebuilt when a structure falls). Units walk downhill on their field, plus **separation** (same-team units within 3.5 studs push apart) and a slowly changing sideways **wander**, so the horde fans out over open ground, flows around obstacles and converges on the openings. Each zombie picks one base opening at spawn, weighted 1/distance² (about 55% / 45% from either core), so neither core feeds only one opening. Our minions follow a field to the nearest zombie core. A unit with an enemy in aggro range leaves the field, closes in while still spreading out and surrounds the target instead of stacking; units standing to fight shuffle apart if packed.
- **Reuse:** the prototype minion system (`src/server/Minions.server.luau`) becomes the enemy system: wave spawner with an alive cap *(carried: cap 30)*, waypoint walking, sticky targets, 12-stud / 3 s leash, retaliation within 20 studs, smooth turning (270°/s), melee and ranged types (the archer's draw-then-arrow attack, damage on arrival), full player size. Pathing (2026-10-20): flow fields when no enemy; when chasing an enemy, straight only if the line is open on the grid, else a short A* detour; drift never steers into obstacles; a unit that stops progressing steers plainly for 2.5 s.
- **Targets:** enemies attack players, walls (and other built structures) and the Base Core. Current rule: nearest enemy of any kind in aggro range (see Zombie aggro), else walk the flow field toward the core. Whether built walls/turrets/traps change this stays **OPEN** until T13.
- **Waves:** number of waves per map, composition, spacing and scaling **OPEN**. Waves start after the build phase timer ends (or when all players ready up — **OPEN**).
- **Boss:** the final wave of the full version is a boss. Design **OPEN**.
- Enemy types beyond melee and ranged: **OPEN**.

## Gold, building and repair
- **Gold** (owner 2026-10-20): per player, earned by landing the killing hit on a zombie with your hero (`Config.Gold.PerLastHit`, placeholder 1). Shown top right (`src/client/GoldCounter.client.luau`), with a gold "+1" rising from the zombie you finished (`GoldGained` event, sent only to you); server-owned in `src/server/Gold.server.luau` (Player attribute `Gold`). Nothing to spend it on yet; kept for the session. Amounts and resetting per mission come with the mission flow.
- **Build phase:** between waves; duration **OPEN**. Players spend Gold at pre-placed build nodes on:
  - **Walls:** block enemies; enemies attack them.
  - **Turrets:** fire at enemies. Reuse the prototype tower AI in `src/server/Structures.server.luau` *(carried: range 28, 60 damage every 1 s, glowing shot 80 studs/s with damage on arrival, keeps its target while in range)*, its visuals (`TowerVisuals.server.luau`, `TowerEffects.client.luau`, the knight `assets/TowerModel.rbxmx` as possible turret art) and the range ring.
  - **Traps:** **OPEN** (types, trigger, damage).
- **Repair:** players spend Gold (or time — **OPEN**) to restore damaged walls/turrets/traps (and the core? **OPEN**) before the next wave. Repair during waves **OPEN**.
- Catalogue, prices, health and node layout **OPEN**.
- Prototype rules not used in the new design (code still present until replaced): tower damage ramp on heroes, backdoor protection, minion lifetime despawn (180 s; whether enemies should live until killed is **OPEN**). The tower -> inhibitor -> core order and the inhibitor bonus are back for now (2026-10-20). Lanes and waypoint routes are gone (swarm movement replaced them).

## Base Core
Single core per mission *(carried: core health 1200)*. Does not attack. Its health bar is always visible on the HUD. When it reaches 0 the mission is lost immediately; result committed once.

## Persistent progression
- Saved per player (DataStores; first persistence in the project): credits, per-hero level and XP, owned gear (and heroes, if they are bought), current loadout per hero.
- **Credits** earned per mission (amount by result/map/difficulty **OPEN**), spent in the hub on weapons and gear.
- **Hero XP** per mission for the hero played; levels unlock harder maps with better rewards. Curves and caps **OPEN**.
- Saving must handle DataStore failures without losing or duplicating progress (session lock / retry rules decided in T20).

## HUD and presentation (PC first)
- **All UI follows the design system in `docs/11_UI_DESIGN.md`** (owner decision 2026-10-21): Builder Sans, one see-through dark surface, gold accent for progress and rewards only, green/red for health meaning only, thin bars with labels above, 4 px spacing. Values in `src/shared/UiTheme.luau`.
- **Kept from the prototype:** overhead name + segmented health bar on each player, health bars on enemies and structures within 60 studs of your character *(carried)*, damage numbers and hit flash, MLBB-style announcement banner (to be reused for "WAVE 3 INCOMING", "BUILD PHASE", "CORE UNDER ATTACK", win/loss).
- **Done (2026-10-20):** XP bar under the health bar and a live level badge, both reading the server's `Level` / `Xp` / `XpToNext` Player attributes; minimap bottom-right.
- **In-match levels, rogue-like (owner 2026-10-20, placeholder; more work later):** every zombie that dies, killed by anyone, gives **every player** 1 XP (owner 2026-10-20: no last hit needed). **30 XP per level** for now (`Config.Hero.Leveling`). Each level-up gives one upgrade pick: the level badge gets a gold outline and a "U" hint. **Character Menu (owner spec 2026-10-20, `src/client/CharacterMenu.client.luau`):** U opens/closes it at any time (or click the gold badge); the mouse is freed while it is open, the game keeps running. Left panel (~38%): player name, hero name, level, gold XP bar with "xp / next XP", and the real stats from server attributes (Max Health, Attack Damage, Move Speed, Attack Speed per second, Attack Range in studs; no Defense, it does not exist). Right panel (~62%): with a pick waiting, three cards (icon, name, x1.5, one-line effect, before -> after preview; click or 1/2/3; "N picks left" when several are queued); otherwise a note with the next pick's level and XP to go. **Picking closes the menu** (owner 2026-10-20). Below the stats: **SKILLS** (Lightning Bolt basic attack plus three locked slots) and **TALENTS** (cards taken, as icons with a gold xN chip); hovering any of them shows a tooltip with details (skill damage/range/cooldown; talent effect, picks, total multiplier, current value). Drawn at 125% (shrinks on small screens). Cards (owner 2026-10-21): three slots; the first offer is the three **ability unlocks** (slot 1 Lightning Strike, 2 Storm Dash, 3 Ball Lightning); picking a slot refills only that slot with a random talent not on offer (stat talents **Damage x1.5** (also scales abilities), **Move Speed x1.5**, **Health x1.5**; unlocked abilities are not offered again). **25 levels max**, no masteries. Zeus's abilities: **1 Lightning Strike** (hold to show the exact area on the ground, release: area damage, short stun, then slow), **2 Storm Dash** (dash where you face, knockback where you land), **3 Ball Lightning** (charge 1 s, single target: heavy damage and long stun); numbers in `Config.Hero.Skills`, details in `docs/10_ZEUS_UPGRADES_PLAN.md`. The HUD shows "xp / next XP" beside the XP bar and the level ring glows gold only while a pick is waiting, with a "LEVEL UP" pill (U). A **target panel** at the top shows the face, name and health of what you aim at. Lightning Strike's aiming area lies on the ground and follows hills (GroundRing). Damage numbers and "+1" gold pop, hold and drift for about 1.5 s. Picks stack multiplicatively and survive death for the session; not saved. Server-owned (`src/server/HeroProgress.luau`, `ChooseUpgrade` remote); UI in `src/client/HeroHud.client.luau` and `src/client/CharacterMenu.client.luau`; the server publishes `Stat*` and `<id>Picks` Player attributes for it. Structure kills give no XP for now. Later levels' XP, more cards and card rarity **OPEN**; a 25-upgrade pool for Zeus is proposed in `docs/10_ZEUS_UPGRADES_PLAN.md` (not built). Stacked Attack damage is capped at the damage gateway's 10,000 per hit.
